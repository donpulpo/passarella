Generative AI put Datamaran’s core value proposition at risk almost overnight. We had to reinvent the platform while still serving, and improving, the one our clients relied on.

<div class="draft">Draft: the moment you realised the risk was real. One concrete scene works best.</div>

## A roadmap that came back every quarter

I built a recurring AI roadmap: stakeholder and client feedback, research on outcomes and opportunities, and recommendations taken to the board. Its first job was to help leadership grasp how material the risk was.

The board invested £500K in generative AI capabilities. We created an innovation lab for agentic development, and shipped around ten generative and agentic AI features a year.

## What I would do earlier

The bottleneck was rarely the technology. Decisions moved at the speed of the leadership team’s understanding. I now invest much earlier in educating executive teams, so that decisions move faster.
